# Video Production Guidelines for SustainAds AI

## Overview
Guidelines for creating compelling 30-second video ads for climate-resilient local businesses, optimized for social media and digital marketing platforms.

## Video Specifications

### Technical Requirements
- **Duration**: 30 seconds (±2 seconds acceptable)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps or 24fps
- **Aspect Ratios**: 
  - 16:9 (YouTube, Facebook, website)
  - 9:16 (Instagram Stories, TikTok, Reels)
  - 1:1 (Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed)
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **File Size**: Under 100MB for easy sharing
- **Color Space**: sRGB

### Platform-Specific Recommendations

#### YouTube
- 16:9 format
- Include captions (80% watch without sound)
- First 3 seconds are critical
- End screen with channel branding

#### Instagram/Facebook
- Square (1:1) or vertical (9:16) for best mobile experience
- Bright, eye-catching visuals
- Text overlays for soundless viewing
- Fast-paced cuts (3-5 second scenes)

#### TikTok
- Vertical (9:16) required
- Native, authentic feel
- Quick hook in first 2 seconds
- Trending music/sounds optional

## Visual Structure

### Scene Breakdown (30-second video)

#### Scene 1: Opening Hook (0-5 seconds)
**Purpose**: Capture attention and establish brand
**Visual Elements**:
- Logo/business name reveal
- Striking hero shot of business or product
- Location establishing shot
- Eye-catching color or movement

**Examples**:
- Coffee shop: Steam rising from latte with logo latte art
- Retail store: Colorful sustainable products arranged artistically
- Restaurant: Chef preparing signature dish

**Transitions**: Quick fade or cut

#### Scene 2: Problem/Context (5-10 seconds)
**Purpose**: Relate to audience needs
**Visual Elements**:
- Show customer pain point or desire
- Community context
- Environmental challenge or opportunity

**Examples**:
- Show customers enjoying experience
- Display sustainable practices in action
- Highlight local community connection

#### Scene 3: Solution/Value Proposition (10-20 seconds)
**Purpose**: Showcase business offerings and sustainability
**Visual Elements**:
- Product/service demonstrations
- Sustainable practices visualization
- Customer testimonials (optional)
- Behind-the-scenes footage
- Key differentiators

**Examples**:
- Time-lapse of farm-to-table process
- Montage of eco-friendly products
- Staff interacting with happy customers
- Sustainable certifications or awards

**Transitions**: Smooth cross-dissolve or match cut

#### Scene 4: Call-to-Action (20-30 seconds)
**Purpose**: Drive viewer action
**Visual Elements**:
- Business location/storefront
- Contact information overlay
- Website/social media handles
- QR code (optional)
- Final brand logo

**Examples**:
- Map animation showing location
- Store hours and contact details
- Social media follow buttons
- Special offer or promotion

**Transitions**: Fade to brand card

## Visual Style Guidelines

### Color Palette
**Primary**: Earth tones and natural colors
- Greens: #2ECC71, #27AE60 (growth, sustainability)
- Blues: #3498DB, #2980B9 (trust, stability)
- Browns/Tans: #8B7355, #D2B48C (organic, natural)

**Secondary**: Brand-specific colors
- Use business brand colors prominently
- Ensure sufficient contrast for readability

**Accents**: Warm, inviting tones
- Orange: #E67E22 (energy, warmth)
- Yellow: #F1C40F (optimism, friendly)

### Typography
**Headings**: Bold, sans-serif
- Montserrat, Poppins, Roboto
- 48-72pt for main titles

**Body Text**: Clean, readable
- Open Sans, Lato, Source Sans Pro
- 24-36pt for subtitles

**Rules**:
- Maximum 3-4 words per text overlay
- High contrast with background
- On screen for minimum 3 seconds
- Avoid text in safe zones (edges)

### Cinematography

#### Camera Movements
- **Static shots**: Product details, logos, text
- **Slow pan/tilt**: Establishing shots, locations
- **Tracking shots**: Following action, process
- **Handheld/stabilized**: Authentic, documentary feel

#### Shot Types
1. **Wide shots**: Establish location and context
2. **Medium shots**: Show people and interaction
3. **Close-ups**: Product details, emotional moments
4. **Macro shots**: Textures, ingredients, craftsmanship

#### Lighting
- Natural lighting preferred for authentic feel
- Warm, soft lighting for inviting atmosphere
- Avoid harsh shadows
- Golden hour for outdoor shots

### B-Roll Footage Categories

#### Essential Elements
1. **Business Exterior**
   - Storefront/entrance
   - Signage
   - Surrounding neighborhood
   - Parking/accessibility

2. **Business Interior**
   - Welcoming atmosphere
   - Products/services displayed
   - Clean, organized space
   - Unique design elements

3. **Products/Services**
   - Hero products
   - Process/creation
   - Packaging
   - Quality details

4. **People**
   - Owners/founders
   - Staff at work
   - Happy customers
   - Community interactions

5. **Sustainability Elements**
   - Recycling/composting
   - Solar panels/green tech
   - Local sourcing
   - Eco-friendly packaging
   - Community gardens

6. **Community Connection**
   - Local events
   - Partnerships
   - Customer testimonials
   - Neighborhood scenes

## Motion Graphics & Animation

### Text Animations
- **Fade in/out**: Clean and professional
- **Slide in**: Dynamic and modern
- **Typewriter effect**: Emphasis on key phrases
- **Scale up**: Draw attention to important info

### Logo Animation
- 2-3 second reveal
- Clean, simple motion
- Consistent with brand identity

### Transitions
- **Cuts**: Fast-paced, energetic content
- **Cross-dissolve**: Smooth, professional
- **Match cut**: Creative, storytelling
- **Wipe**: Playful, modern

### Lower Thirds
- Business name
- Location
- Website/contact
- Certifications
- Special offers

## Audio-Visual Sync

### Music Selection
**Criteria**:
- Upbeat and positive
- Not overwhelming voiceover
- Matches brand personality
- Royalty-free or licensed

**Genres**:
- Acoustic/Folk: Organic, authentic
- Indie Pop: Modern, friendly
- Light Electronic: Professional, innovative
- Instrumental: Versatile, clean

**Volume Levels**:
- Music: -20dB to -15dB
- Voiceover: -12dB to -8dB
- Sound effects: -18dB to -15dB

### Sound Effects
Use sparingly:
- Subtle whoosh for transitions
- Soft ding for key points
- Natural ambient sounds (café chatter, nature)

### Voiceover Integration
- Start voice 1-2 seconds into video
- Sync key words with visual emphasis
- Pause for visual moments
- End voice 2-3 seconds before video ends

## Editing Workflow

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Import Assets**
   - Video footage (4K preferred, export 1080p)
   - Audio files (voiceover, music)
   - Graphics (logo, text elements)
   - Brand assets

2. **Rough Cut**
   - Arrange clips to match script
   - Establish pacing and flow
   - Remove unnecessary footage
   - Verify 30-second duration

3. **Fine Cut**
   - Refine transitions
   - Color correction
   - Audio mixing
   - Add text overlays and graphics

4. **Effects & Motion Graphics**
   - Animate text
   - Add logo animation
   - Apply subtle effects
   - Insert lower thirds

5. **Final Review**
   - Check audio levels
   - Verify all text is readable
   - Ensure smooth transitions
   - Test on different devices

6. **Export**
   - Multiple aspect ratios if needed
   - Optimize for platforms
   - Create thumbnail images
   - Generate captions/subtitles

### Editing Software Recommendations
- **Professional**: Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro X
- **Intermediate**: DaVinci Resolve, Filmora
- **Beginner**: iMovie, Clipchamp
- **Online**: Canva Video, Adobe Express

## Accessibility Requirements

### Captions/Subtitles
- Include on all videos (80% watch muted)
- White text with black outline or background
- Large, readable font (minimum 24pt)
- Sync accurately with audio
- Include sound effects descriptions

### Color Contrast
- Minimum 4.5:1 ratio for text
- Test for colorblind accessibility
- Avoid relying solely on color to convey information

### Audio Descriptions
- For visually impaired users
- Describe key visual elements
- Include in extended version if possible

## Platform Upload Guidelines

### File Naming Convention
`BusinessName_Platform_AspectRatio_Date.mp4`
Example: `GreenCafe_Instagram_1x1_20241109.mp4`

### Metadata
- Title: Include business name and key benefit
- Description: Brief, keyword-rich (sustainability, local, eco-friendly)
- Tags: Relevant to industry and sustainability
- Thumbnail: Eye-catching frame from video

### Publishing Best Practices
- Schedule for peak engagement times
- Include strong caption with CTA
- Add relevant hashtags (#ShopLocal, #Sustainable, #EcoFriendly)
- Tag business location
- Encourage shares and engagement
- Monitor comments and respond
